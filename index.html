Arte · Arquitetura · Ambiente

Three tools for drawing with the cell that builds the LINA logo. Whatever you make follows the logo's own corner rule, so it always belongs to the family.

Cell editor

Place and drag cells on a grid to draw letters, marks and icons. Every change redraws the outline, and you can export it as a clean SVG.

Open the editor

Texture generator

Seeded random patterns built from the same cells. Choose the pattern, the seed and how much blank space it leaves, then export SVG or PNG.

Open the generator Click the pattern to see another.

Image filter

Turn a photo into a halftone of LINA dots, stems or cells, with the original beside it. Your image never leaves your computer.

Open the filter

The rule

Every shape here is a union of square cells. Its outline is then rounded by one rule, with a radius of a quarter cell, and nothing else. The image filter adds one exception, the half-cell dot, which the same rule turns into a circle.
